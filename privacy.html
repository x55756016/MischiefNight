Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Oct 07, 2026

1. Introduction

This game respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes what data we collect, how we use, store and protect information when you use this game.

No account registration or login is required. We do NOT collect your name, phone number, email, ID number or any personal identity information.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect anonymous data which will NOT be linked to your real identity:

  • IDFA (Advertising Identifier): Used for ad display, ad performance statistics and anti-cheat purposes.
  • Product interaction data: Game launch events, button clicks and in-game player behavior.
  • Ad data: Ad impressions and ad click records.
  • Crash data: Game crash logs for bug diagnosis and crash fixes.
  • Performance data: Startup time, frame rate, stuttering and memory metrics to optimize game experience.

We do NOT collect location data, photos, camera access, microphone, contacts, health data, biometric data or payment information.

3. Purpose of Data Use

  • Third-party advertising: Serve advertisements via LevelPlay (Unity Ads).
  • Data analytics: Analyze player behavior, ad conversion and game retention to evaluate game features.
  • App functionality and stability: Anti-cheat, server security verification, crash repair and game performance optimization.

4. Data Sharing

Anonymous data is shared only with our advertising service provider: LevelPlay (Unity Ads).

The third-party provider uses data only in accordance with this Privacy Policy and Apple privacy rules.

Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We will never sell or rent your data to any other third parties.

5. Tracking

Advertising-related data (IDFA and ad records) may be used by the ad SDK for cross-app tracking to deliver personalized ads.

You can disable tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Disabling will stop IDFA collection. This only affects personalized ads, core gameplay will remain unaffected.

6. Data Storage

Anonymous data is stored only for the necessary period and automatically deleted after expiration. We will not associate anonymous data with your real identity.

7. Children’s Privacy

This game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Please contact us if you believe we have mistakenly collected child data.

8. Your Rights

You may disable app tracking permission in iOS system settings. Contact us for questions regarding data collection.
